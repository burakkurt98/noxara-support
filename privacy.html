NOXARA Privacy Policy

Effective 1 October 2026 · Controller: Burak Kurt · Contact: burakkurt72@gmail.com

This policy describes how the NOXARA mobile game handles data.

Data stored on your device

NOXARA stores game progress, a hero name you choose, in-game balances, achievements and settings locally on your device. It does not require an account or offer cloud save. Deleting the app or its data removes this local save, subject to any device backup you control.

Optional in-app purchases

If you use the in-game shop, Apple processes payment through the App Store; NOXARA does not receive your card or bank details. The game uses Unity In-App Purchasing to fetch products and confirm purchases. Unity states that its IAP 5.4+ service may process a player or installation identifier, device information, session identifiers, country and purchase records to provide the service. Information on Unity's data practices and requests is available in Unity's IAP privacy documentation and Unity's game player privacy policy. Apple's handling of App Store purchases is described in Apple's App Store privacy notice.

Support messages

If you contact support, we receive the email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer and resolve your request. Please avoid sending passwords or payment card data. We retain correspondence only as needed to handle the request and applicable obligations; you may request deletion where available.

Advertising and tracking

NOXARA does not include an advertising SDK and does not use personal data for cross-app advertising tracking. The current game does not include a separate analytics SDK.

Your choices and requests

You can remove the local game save by deleting the app and its data. For privacy questions, access or deletion requests concerning information handled by us, contact burakkurt72@gmail.com. For purchase and platform data, Apple or Unity may handle a request under their own policies. We will update this page if NOXARA's data practices materially change.
