NOXARA Support

Help for the NOXARA mobile game. For questions, bugs or purchase problems, email burakkurt72@gmail.com.

Please include your device model, iOS version, game version and a short description of what happened. You may attach a screenshot. Please do not send passwords or payment card details.

Frequently asked questions

How is progress saved?

Progress and settings are saved on your device. Reinstalling the game or deleting its local data may remove your progress. There is currently no account or cloud save.

How do purchases work?

Optional in-game currency packs are sold through the App Store when available. Apple handles payment. If a completed purchase has not appeared, reopen the game while connected to the internet and then contact support with the product name and approximate purchase time. Do not email full payment details.

How do I change the language or sound?

Open Settings in the game to change the language, music and sound effects.
